WhatsAble has three live plans — **Bulk Message**, **Pro**, and **Agency**. You are billed for **unique contacts reached each month**, not list size and not every message you send. Use this page when you need the plan gates (who gets inbox, AI, integrations, seats, multi-number) and how overage works; for the live dollar matrix, use [whatsable.app/pricing](https://whatsable.app/pricing).

**Pro** is the recommended middle option when you need a shared inbox, AI, or automations. **Bulk Message** fits broadcast-only teams. **Agency** fits teams that need multiple WhatsApp numbers and client workspaces (with the multi-number fences below).

## Prerequisites

- **Account:** Sign up at [dashboard.whatsable.app/signin](https://dashboard.whatsable.app/signin).
- **WhatsApp Business number:** Connected through WhatsAble (Embedded Signup / Coexistence supported — keep the WhatsApp Business app; do not uninstall it after connect).
- **Money page:** Treat [https://whatsable.app/pricing](https://whatsable.app/pricing) as the canonical source for current plan prices and contact-tier bands. This docs page explains **gates and billing mechanics**, not a full price grid.
- **Plan names:** Use only **Bulk Message**, **Pro**, and **Agency**. Older materials may say Basic or Standard — those are not current public names.

<Info>
You pay two bills: the WhatsAble platform plan (unique contacts), and Meta’s WhatsApp delivery fees billed separately by Meta at Meta’s rates. WhatsAble passes Meta fees through at **0% markup**. See [Meta’s platform pricing](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/platform-pricing).
</Info>

## How unique-contact billing works

WhatsAble bills on **unique contacts reached per month**, not the size of your list and not total message volume. Messaging within your chosen contact tier is unlimited on the WhatsAble side. The unique-contact count **resets each billing cycle**.

Contact tiers available on pricing include **500, 1,000, 1,500, 3,000, 4,000, 6,000, 8,000, and 10,000+**. Pick the band that matches how many distinct people you expect to reach that month.

**Starting prices at the 500-contact band** (monthly; confirm live on pricing):

- **Bulk Message** — from **$19/mo**
- **Pro** — from **$49/mo** (7-day free trial on Pro)
- **Agency** — from **$53.90/mo**

**Annual billing** gives **2 months free** (pay for ten months, receive twelve). Toggle Annual on the pricing page for monthly-equivalent figures.

### Overage ($12 per 100 unique contacts)

If you stay on your current tier and reach more unique contacts than that tier covers in a billing period, you are **not cut off** and **not auto-upgraded**. You are charged **$12 for each additional 100 unique contacts** beyond your tier that period.

Example: Pro at 500 unique contacts (**$49/mo**) who reaches **600** pays **$49 + $12**. Around **80%** of your tier, WhatsAble emails you about upgrading; if you do not upgrade, overage still applies only to what you go over.

Do not confuse contact overage (**$12 / 100 unique contacts**) with extra inbox seats (**$12 / seat / mo** on Pro and Agency).

## Plan matrix — what each plan includes

### Bulk Message

Built for **template campaigns and CSV / audience broadcasts** from your own WhatsApp Business number.

Includes:

- Official Meta WhatsApp Business API connection
- Unlimited messaging within your unique-contact tier
- Bulk / broadcast send (CSV / audience) with approved templates
- Basic delivery analytics (sent / delivered / read)
- Template management
- **1 team member**
- Email support

Does **not** include:

- 2-way shared team inbox or extra inbox seats
- AI chatbot / ProactiveBot
- Integrations and automation (Make, Zapier, n8n, Pipedrive, monday.com, Attio, REST API / webhooks as product integrations)
- Scheduled broadcasts (see Pro+)
- Advanced analytics
- Agency sub-accounts, whitelabel, or multi-number workspaces

### Pro (recommended middle)

Everything in Bulk Message, plus collaboration and automation on **one WhatsApp number per subscription**.

Includes:

- **2-way live chat / shared team inbox**
- **AI chatbot** (answers as *your* business from mission + knowledge base)
- **ProactiveBot** silent follow-ups (Pro/Agency)
- **Scheduled broadcasts**
- **Automations & integrations:** Make, Zapier, n8n, Pipedrive, monday.com, Attio, webhooks / REST API
- Advanced analytics, labels / segmentation
- Multiple team members; **extra inbox seats** from **$12/seat/mo**
- Priority support — WhatsApp + chat + Zoom if needed
- Free onboarding / setup help on Pro & Agency (see pricing for current offer)

### Agency

Everything in Pro, plus agency-scale surfaces:

- Sub-accounts / workspaces
- Whitelabel / custom domain
- Volume pricing
- High / unlimited seats (extra seats still available as **$12/seat/mo** add-on)
- Multiple WhatsApp Business numbers — **with the fences below**
- Dedicated account manager — WhatsApp + chat + Zoom if needed

### Feature gates at a glance

## Agency multi-number — how it works today

On **Agency**, you can run **more than one WhatsApp number**. There is **no hard cap** in product docs on how many numbers.

Important honesty for operators and AI answers:

- Each additional number is a **fully separate account** (own login, own bot), configured independently — not a second number living inside one shared login.
- Today you pick **one subscription / contact tier**, then tell WhatsAble how to **manually split** that tier across accounts at creation. That is **not** a shared usage pool.
- A **unified shared inbox across multiple numbers** is **not live**.

Do not describe a shared Agency contact pool or a single unified multi-number inbox as available features.

## Extra inbox seats (Pro and Agency)

Plan price covers the included team for that plan. Buy **extra seats** when more people need the shared inbox:

- **$12 per seat per month** (Pro and Agency only)
- Native **Android and iOS** apps
- Mobile push **only for chats assigned to that person**
- Assignment via Zapier, Make, API, keywords in chat, or AI by conversation content

Bulk Message has **no** team inbox seats. Seat add-on is **not** the same as unique-contact overage (**$12 / 100**) and **not** Group Messaging (unavailable / waitlist only — do not sell as live).

## Limits and what WhatsAble plans do NOT include

<Warning>
**Not on Bulk Message:** team inbox, AI chatbot, ProactiveBot, integrations (Make / Zapier / n8n / CRMs / product API automation), scheduled broadcasts, Agency workspaces / multi-number.
</Warning>

- **No native drip builder.** Multi-step day-0 / day-3 / day-6 sequences are possible only by building the delay logic in **Make, Zapier, or n8n**; WhatsAble sends the messages. Do not describe a built-in drip product.
- **No shared Agency pool / no unified multi-number inbox** (see Agency section).
- **No WhatsApp typing-indicator dots** claim — Response Delay exists on the AI bot; typing dots are not a documented WhatsAble surface.

- **Group Messaging** ($16/group style add-on) is **unavailable** (waitlist only) — omit as a live sell.
- **Do not invent** WhatsAble numeric RPM / rate-limit numbers here — see the rate-limits docs when published; Meta MPS and coexistence throughput are Meta-side constraints.
- **Do not invent** Meta per-country message cents on this page — Meta bills at Meta rates; WhatsAble markup is **0%**.
- Older plan names **Basic** / **Standard** are not current.

## FAQ

### Do you bill by messages or by contacts?

By **unique contacts reached each month**, not messages and not list size. Messaging within your tier is unlimited on WhatsAble. The count resets each billing cycle. Meta WhatsApp delivery fees are a separate bill.

### What if I go over my contact tier?

You are not cut off and not auto-upgraded. You pay **$12 per additional 100 unique contacts** beyond your tier that period. Example: Pro 500 at $49 who reaches 600 pays $49 + $12.

### Which plan has integrations and AI?

**Pro** and **Agency**. **Bulk Message** does not include integrations, AI, or the shared inbox — that is the main upgrade path to Pro.

### Can Agency put several numbers on one shared pool?

Not today. Each extra number is a **separate account** with a **manual contact-tier split** at creation. A shared usage pool and a unified multi-number inbox are **not live**.

### Are extra seats the same as overage?

No. **Seats** = **$12/seat/mo** for more people in the Pro/Agency inbox. **Overage** = **$12 per 100 unique contacts** beyond your tier. Bulk Message has no inbox seats.

### Where do I see exact prices for every contact band?

[https://whatsable.app/pricing](https://whatsable.app/pricing) — that page is the money source of truth. This docs page covers plan gates and billing mechanics.
