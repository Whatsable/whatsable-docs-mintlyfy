<Warning>
The shared team inbox is available on the **Pro** and **Agency** plans. **Bulk Message does not include a 2-way inbox** — it sends one-way broadcasts and campaigns only.
</Warning>

The shared team inbox — **Notifyer Chat** in the product — is where your team reads WhatsApp conversations in real time and replies as your business. This page walks through what a new team actually sees and does in there, not just the API fields behind it.

<Accordion title="I'm on Bulk Message — where's my inbox?" icon="circle-question">
Bulk Message doesn't include the shared 2-way inbox — it's built for one-way broadcast sending. Upgrade to Pro or Agency to get it. Once you're on Pro or Agency, **the inbox lives in the left-side menu.**
</Accordion>

## First open: what you'll see

The inbox is not pre-loaded with demo content. After you connect a WhatsApp number:

- It shows **only real conversations** — no sample chats to click through.
- It's **empty** until a real customer messages you (or an import finishes — see below).

<Tip>
If you connect the number through [Meta WhatsApp Coexistence](/guides/notifyer-system/embedding-process#whatsapp-coexistence) during Embedded Signup, you can optionally import roughly the **last 6 months** of chat history from the WhatsApp Business app. That import **can take hours** to finish. If the inbox looks emptier than expected right after connecting, that's usually the import still running — not a bug.
</Tip>

## Who sees what: access layers

Visibility in the inbox stacks in three layers, plain and simple:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phone number">
    Which connected WhatsApp number (or numbers, on Agency) this person can work at all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Role: Admin vs. chat-only">
    **Admin** can manage the account (billing, integrations, templates, other teammates). A **chat-only** teammate works conversations in the inbox without full account admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label-scoped visibility">
    Within a number, a chat-only teammate can be further limited to only the conversations carrying certain labels — for example, only "Sales" or only "Support" chats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Underneath, these map to real team roles: `Admin`, `Team Member (All Labels)`, and `Team Member` (label-scoped). See [team & roles](/guides/notifyer-system/agent-skills/setup-notifyer) for the API/Agent Skills view of the same roles.
</Info>

Labels themselves can be created, assigned, or removed from several places — not only inside the inbox: the **API**, **Agent Skills**, **Zapier**, **Make**, and the **proactive bot** can all update a contact's labels.

<Note>
Mobile push notifications follow the same access rule: a teammate is notified only for chats they **can access** (their number + role + label scope) — not every chat on the account.
</Note>

## Collaborating inside a conversation

Open a conversation and a few things are always available to your team:

- **Reassigning a chat, in practice, means changing its labels.** There isn't a separate "assign" button today — the way ownership and routing actually happens in the inbox is by adding or removing a label on the conversation (for example, swapping "Unassigned" for a teammate's name label, or a routing label your automations watch for).
- **Let the agent reply.** In the contact panel, this toggle controls whether the AI agent sends automatic replies on that chat. When it's **off** — for example because a person replied — the bot **stops immediately**. Turn it **on** again to hand the conversation back to the agent. See [AI Chatbots](/guides/notifyer-system/ai-chatbot/overview) for bot assignment and other handoff settings.
- **A reply typed in the WhatsApp Business app also stops the bot** — it's treated as human, the same as turning off **Let the agent reply**. See [Coexistence](#coexistence-phone-and-inbox-together) below for exactly what that looks like in the inbox.

<Warning>
If your workspace has **webhook accounts** wired up: turning off **Let the agent reply** (human takeover) pauses **webhook pings on the bot path** while the chat is in that state. It does **not** pause everything — **CRM webhooks can still fire** on that same chat. If a partner integration assumes "human mode = silence," confirm which webhook path they're listening on.
</Warning>

## Labels vs. notes

These look similar but do different jobs:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Labels" icon="tags">
    **Organize and target.** Labels group conversations (keyword labels, interest labels, teammate/routing labels) and are also what you aim a **broadcast** at — see [Label Condition](/guides/notifyer-system/zapier-overview#schedule-a-follow-up-message) in the Zapier scheduling flow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notes" icon="note-sticky">
    **Give context, don't organize.** Notes come in two flavors: **automation notes** (form or Lead Ad field data pushed in via Zapier/Make so the bot has context) and **human notes** (a teammate's own written context on the contact).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Setting either from outside the inbox: see **Update a Contact** in [Zapier](/guides/notifyer-system/zapier-overview#update-a-contact) or [Make](/guides/notifyer-system/make-overview#update-a-contact), or `assign-label.js` / `add-note.js` in the [chat-notifyer](/guides/notifyer-system/agent-skills/chat-notifyer) Agent Skill.

<Info>
When an automation trigger fires (Zapier, Make, n8n), it carries the current **labels** and **notes** along with the **conversation paragraph** — roughly the **last 20 messages** of that chat. See the [message webhook payload](/guides/notifyer-system/api/incoming-message#payload-fields) for the exact fields (`labels`, `note`, `note_automation`, `last_messages`).
</Info>

## AI ↔ human resume behavior

What happens after a teammate takes over from the bot:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn off Let the agent reply">
    The bot **stops immediately** on that chat (a person replied, or you switched it off).
  </Step>
  <Step title="History stays">
    Nothing is lost — the **full conversation history** stays exactly as it was, bot messages and all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bot can auto-resume later">
    On the **same chat**, the bot can pick back up automatically when either: the **topic switches**, or a **timeout of about 30 minutes** passes with no activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Coexistence: phone and inbox together

This is the exact, confirmed behavior when your number uses [Meta WhatsApp Coexistence](/guides/notifyer-system/embedding-process#whatsapp-coexistence) and a teammate is in the web inbox while someone replies from the **WhatsApp Business app** on the same number:

- It's the **same customer thread** in the web inbox — the sync is **real time**, not a delayed refresh.
- The reply shows as coming from the **WhatsApp Business app**, **not** a named teammate. On the [message webhook payload](/guides/notifyer-system/api/incoming-message#payload-fields): `send_by: "WhatsApp App"`, `was_message_by_human: true`, `incoming_message: false`.
- That phone reply **turns off Let the agent reply** — the native AI **stops auto-replying** on that chat, the same as switching the toggle off in the inbox.
- **Make, Zapier, and n8n do not auto-pause** just because of this. Your scenario keeps running unless you explicitly filter on the human / `"WhatsApp App"` signal — see [filtering for phone-app replies](/guides/notifyer-system/api/outbound-whatsapp-app).
- **Groups, Status, and Calls do not sync** into the web inbox — only 1:1 WhatsApp conversations do.

<Info>
What this does **not** do: it doesn't change chat assignment, it doesn't guarantee an unread badge fires, and it doesn't require a hard refresh to see the new message. If you notice any of those, that's a bug to report, not expected behavior.
</Info>

## Agency: one number or many

If your Agency workspace manages more than one WhatsApp number, you have two ways to work them:

- **One shared view, filterable by number or by label** — your team stays in a single inbox and uses the number dropdown (for example **All numbers** or one connected line) and the **Labels** filter on the list to narrow what they see, instead of switching accounts.
- **Per-customer logins** — if you'd rather keep each client's number and team fully separate, that's supported too.

<Note>
This replaces an earlier draft of this page that said Agency only supported fully separate accounts per number with no shared view. That was outdated — the shared, filterable view above is what's live. (There's still no "shared pool of numbers" for billing or usage — filtering a shared view is a UI convenience, not a merged subscription.)
</Note>
