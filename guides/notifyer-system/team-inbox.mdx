<Warning>
The shared team inbox is available on the **Pro** and **Agency** plans. **Bulk Message does not include a 2-way inbox** — it sends one-way broadcasts and campaigns only. Upgrade on the [Pricing](https://whatsable.app/pricing) page to unlock it.
</Warning>

## What is the shared team inbox?

The shared team inbox is the **2-way, real-time WhatsApp conversation view** inside WhatsAble — called **Notifyer Chat** in the product (`chat.notifyer-systems.com`). It is where your team reads incoming customer messages and replies **as your connected WhatsApp number**, side by side with your teammates.

| Plan | Shared team inbox |
|------|--------------------|
| Bulk Message | ✗ Not included — broadcast/CSV sending only |
| Pro | ✓ Full 2-way inbox |
| Agency | ✓ Full 2-way inbox |

<Note>
On Agency, connecting more than one WhatsApp number creates a **separate account** for each number, each with its own login and its own inbox. There is currently no single unified inbox that shows conversations from multiple connected numbers at once.
</Note>

## 2-way live inbox

Unlike a one-way broadcast list, every conversation in the shared inbox is live in both directions:

- **Incoming** — a customer's WhatsApp message appears in the inbox in real time, next to their full conversation.
- **Outgoing** — anyone on your team can open that conversation and reply immediately, with the reply delivered over WhatsApp as your business.

This 2-way behaviour is exactly what the [message webhook payload](/guides/notifyer-system/api/incoming-message) mirrors outside the UI: `incoming_message: true` for customer messages, `incoming_message: false` for anything your business sends back — whether that reply came from the inbox, the WhatsApp Business app, or an automation.

## Reply as the business

When a teammate replies from the shared inbox, the message goes out from your connected WhatsApp Business number — the customer sees a reply from your business, not from an individual's personal WhatsApp number.

The same conversation stays consistent no matter which surface your team uses to reply:

You do not have to choose one surface. Coexistence means the inbox, the WhatsApp Business app on a phone, and automations (Make, Zapier, n8n, the API) can all reply on the same connected number, and every reply shows up in the same conversation history.
</Info>

## Conversation history

Every conversation in the shared inbox keeps a running history regardless of which surface sent or received each message:

- The inbox shows the full back-and-forth for a contact, including messages sent from the inbox itself, the WhatsApp Business app, bots, and automations.
- Outside the UI, the same history is available as `last_messages` (recent messages, newest activity included) and `conversation_paragraph` (a readable transcript) on the [message webhook payload](/guides/notifyer-system/api/incoming-message#payload-fields).
- **Notes** (`note`, `note_automation`) and **labels** (`labels`) attached to a contact travel with the conversation and are visible in the inbox alongside the history — see **Update a Contact** in [Zapier](/guides/notifyer-system/zapier-overview#update-a-contact) or [Make](/guides/notifyer-system/make-overview#update-a-contact) for how notes and labels get set from outside the inbox.

## Teammate collaboration

The shared inbox is built for more than one person to work the same set of conversations:

- **AI ↔ human handoff.** If you use the [AI chatbot](/guides/notifyer-system/ai-chatbot/overview), each conversation has a **Human Agent ↔ AI Assistant** toggle in the inbox contact panel. Flip it to take over a conversation from the bot, or back to let the bot resume.
- **Labels and notes.** Teammates can tag conversations with labels and leave contact notes directly from the inbox to organize work and give the AI more context.
- **Chat assignment.** A conversation can be assigned to a specific teammate so the right person owns the thread. Today this is done through automation — [Zapier](/guides/notifyer-system/zapier-overview#update-a-contact), [Make](/guides/notifyer-system/make-overview), the API, keyword rules, or AI-based content matching — rather than a manual "assign" button inside the inbox itself.

<Note>
Chat assignment and labels have their own automation field references (Zapier and Make's **Update a Contact** action). This page covers how the shared inbox uses assignment and labels for collaboration; it is not the full assignment/labels reference.
</Note>

## What the shared inbox is not

To avoid confusion when comparing plans or automation options:

- It is **not** a broadcast tool. Bulk Message's CSV/broadcast sending is one-way and does not include this 2-way inbox.
- It does **not** currently unify multiple connected WhatsApp numbers into one inbox on Agency — see the note above.
- It does **not** hide unassigned conversations from teammates. Assignment routes ownership; it does not remove other chats from view.
