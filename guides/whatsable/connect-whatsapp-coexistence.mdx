# Connect WhatsApp Business to WhatsAble (Embedded Signup + Coexistence)

Keep the WhatsApp Business app on your phone and add WhatsAble on the same number. Meta calls this **Coexistence**. This guide covers what Coexistence actually means, what to check before you start Embedded Signup, the signup flow at a high level, and what changes (and what doesn't) once you're connected.

## What Coexistence means

Meta Coexistence lets one WhatsApp number run in two places at the same time: the **WhatsApp Business app** on your phone, and **WhatsAble** in the background.

- You keep the WhatsApp Business app installed and can keep using it. Connecting to WhatsAble does not require removing it.
- Messages sync **both ways**. A reply typed on your phone reaches WhatsAble, and a message sent from WhatsAble reaches your phone.
- At the QR / Coexistence step, you'll get the option to **share chat history**. Meta may label it **"From last 6 months"**. Choosing it can bring roughly the **last 6 months** of your **1:1** chats into WhatsAble (about 180 days), per Meta's own docs.
- **Groups**, **Status**, and **Calls** stay on the WhatsApp Business app. They are **not** synced to WhatsAble, no matter what you choose at the chat history step.
- If you skip sharing chat history, that's fine too. Only new messages sync from that point forward.

<Warning>
Do not uninstall the WhatsApp Business app after you connect. Coexistence depends on the app staying installed on that number.
</Warning>

## Before you start

<Check>
A **Meta Business Portfolio**. Embedded Signup requires a Business Portfolio, not a personal Facebook profile. You can create one during the flow, but having one ready first is faster. For the website field, a business website is fine, but an Instagram or Facebook page URL also works if you don't have one yet.
</Check>

<Check>
A phone number with **some recent activity** on it. A brand-new, never-used number may fail Coexistence. The number should also already be registered on the **WhatsApp Business app** (not the consumer WhatsApp app).
</Check>

<Check>
Confirm your number's country isn't restricted. See [country restrictions](#country-restrictions) below.
</Check>

### Country restrictions

Meta doesn't support new WhatsApp Business Platform connections from **Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria**, and **some regions of Ukraine**. If your number is registered in one of these, an existing number can't connect. You'd need a different number outside those restrictions.

### If you're already using an unofficial WhatsApp tool on this number

Turning on Coexistence replaces whatever **unofficial (non-Meta) API tool** was running on that number. Once Coexistence is active, those unofficial tools stop working on it. That's expected.

### Pricing

The phone number itself is **free**. Meta doesn't charge for it. What you pay for is your WhatsAble plan; WhatsAble passes Meta's conversation costs through at **0% markup**. See [pricing](https://whatsable.app/pricing) for current plans.

## Embedded Signup, at a high level

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Connect WhatsApp from WhatsAble">
    From your WhatsAble account, start the Connect WhatsApp flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in with Facebook">
    Meta's Embedded Signup opens. Log in with the Facebook account tied to your Meta Business Portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or create your Business Portfolio">
    Select the Meta Business Portfolio this number belongs to, or create one if you don't have one yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or create a WhatsApp Business Account">
    Choose an existing WhatsApp Business Account, or let Meta create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your phone number">
    Enter the number you want to connect. If Meta detects that number is already active in the WhatsApp Business app, it offers Coexistence instead of a fresh registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code and choose Coexistence">
    Confirm you want to keep the number in the WhatsApp Business app. When asked about your chat history, choose to **share chat history** (Meta may show this as **"From last 6 months"**). That brings roughly the last 6 months of your 1:1 chats into WhatsAble. Groups, Status, and Calls stay on the WhatsApp Business app either way. Skipping this step just means only new messages sync from here on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish verification">
    Complete any remaining verification Meta asks for (business verification, payment method) and wait for confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Embedded Signup runs inside your WhatsAble account. Technical footnote: the underlying console lives at `notifyer.whatsable.app`. You may see that domain during signup; it's WhatsAble, not a separate product.
</Note>

## After you connect

- If your Meta Business Portfolio is already verified, expect to be able to send your first message in about **20 minutes**.
- Both sides stay live: keep replying from the WhatsApp Business app, and send and receive through WhatsAble at the same time.
- Any unofficial API tool that used to run on this number stops working once Coexistence turns on.
- Phone replies typed in the WhatsApp Business app still reach your automations. See [Phone replies in the WhatsApp Business app still hit your webhook](/guides/notifyer-system/api/outbound-whatsapp-app).

## Limits & honesty

- **Throughput is set by Meta, not WhatsAble.** Meta enforces a messages-per-second limit on the Cloud API side for every connected number. WhatsAble doesn't control or raise this limit. It's the same one any Cloud API number gets from Meta.
- **Country restrictions are Meta's, not WhatsAble's.** Numbers registered in Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria, or some regions of Ukraine can't complete Embedded Signup today.
- **Each number connects on its own.** Coexistence, and the connection itself, apply per number. There is no cross-number setup step in this guide.

## Troubleshoot

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Status is stuck on Pending or shows as not Registered">
    Run Embedded Signup again from WhatsAble, using the same number. That clears it most of the time. If it's still stuck after a second attempt, [contact support](mailto:team@whatsable.app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Embedded Signup fails with a vague error">
    Messages like *"temporarily prevented..."* or a generic *"An error occurred. Please try again."* don't say what actually went wrong. To see more detail, go to `business.facebook.com`, open **Settings** (bottom left), then **WhatsApp**, and check your **WhatsApp Accounts**. Click the small white arrow next to **Add** (not the Add button itself), then choose **Add WhatsApp Business App**. That path on Meta's side usually surfaces the real error Embedded Signup was hiding, often a Business Portfolio mismatch or a partner lock (see the two items below). It doesn't always spell out which portfolio is the problem, so you may still need to check your portfolios by hand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your Instagram or Facebook page sits on a different Business Portfolio">
    If the Instagram or Facebook page tied to your account belongs to a different Meta Business Portfolio than the one you're using for WhatsApp, Embedded Signup can fail without a clear reason. Connect using the Business Portfolio that actually owns that page, or move the page into the right portfolio first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Number is already connected to a partner, or Meta says it's not eligible">
    If this number was previously set up through another Meta Business Partner (BSP), that partner link can block Embedded Signup. Go to `business.facebook.com`, then **Settings**, then **WhatsApp**, then the **Partners** tab. Remove the partner tied to this number and wait a few minutes before retrying. While you're there, also confirm you're using the right Business Portfolio and that Business verification isn't the actual holdup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Coexistence isn't offered for my number">
    Confirm the number is registered on the WhatsApp Business app (not the consumer WhatsApp app) and has some recent activity. A brand-new number sometimes needs a day or two of normal use before Meta offers Coexistence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Verification failed or got stuck">
    Wait 24 hours and try again with the same number, or try the other verification method (SMS vs. call). Don't uninstall the WhatsApp Business app while you retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My number's country isn't supported">
    Meta doesn't support new WhatsApp Business Platform connections from Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria, or some regions of Ukraine. [Contact support](mailto:team@whatsable.app) to talk through options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Something went wrong mid-signup and I want to start over">
    A full reset touches your Meta Business Portfolio and WhatsApp Business Account configuration. [Contact support](mailto:team@whatsable.app) rather than trying to undo it yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
